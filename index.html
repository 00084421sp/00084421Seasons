THE 4 SEASONS

Season Description Image
Spring Spring is the season of renewal and growth. Flowers bloom, trees regain their leaves, and the weather gradually warms up. It’s a time when animals become more active and days start to get longer.
Summer Summer is the warmest season of the year. The days are long and sunny and people often enjoy outdoor activities. Plants grow rapidly and many fruits and vegetables are ready to harvest.
Autumn Autumn arrives with the changing colors of leaves. Trees shed their leaves and the days start to shorten. It’s a season often associated with harvest and preparation for winter.
Winter Winter is the coldest season, with shorter days and longer nights. Animals may hibernate and plants go dormant as the environment slows down. It is the darkest time of the year.
